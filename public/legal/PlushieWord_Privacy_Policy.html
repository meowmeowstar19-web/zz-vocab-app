Privacy Policy

PlushieWord · Effective Date: October 7, 2026 · Last Updated: October 7, 2026

 

1. Introduction

PlushieWord ("we," "our," or "us") is a language-learning app at https://plushieword.com, operated by an individual developer based in New York, United States. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: you can learn in Guest Mode without giving us any personal information. If you create an account, we keep only what we need to save your progress and run the app. We do not show ads, and we never sell your information. Players under 13 can make a username account that needs no email address or real name — see Section 8.

2. Guest Mode

In Guest Mode, your learning progress (words you have studied, your review schedule, words you add yourself, and your settings) is stored only in your browser on your device. It is not sent to our servers, and we cannot see it or restore it. If you clear your browser data or switch devices, Guest Mode progress may be lost. If you later create an account, your Guest Mode progress can be moved into it.

Like any visitor, Guest Mode players are included in the usage statistics described in Section 5, which are not linked to a name or email address.

3. Information We Collect from Account Holders

If you create an account, we collect:

Sign-in information — for players 13 and older, your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and basic profile information (such as your name and profile picture) with us. We do not receive your Google password, and these accounts do not use a PlushieWord password. Players under 13 sign in with a username and password instead (see Section 8); passwords are stored only in hashed form, and we cannot see them.

Learning data — the words and phrases you have studied and mastered, your review schedule, words and phrases you add yourself, and your language and learning settings, so that your progress is saved and works across devices.

Avatar — if you choose a picture as your avatar, it is saved only on your device and is never uploaded to us. If you sign in with Google, your Settings page may show your Google profile picture.

Feedback — if you send feedback, we store your message, any screenshots you attach, your account email, your language settings, and your browser type so we can reply and investigate problems.

Technical data — like nearly all websites, our hosting, security, and database providers automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and request times in order to deliver the Service and protect it from abuse. To stop accounts or help requests from being created in bulk, we also keep a scrambled (salted, one-way hashed) version of the IP address that made the request for up to 24 hours, and then delete it.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

Create and manage your account and let you sign in.

Save and sync your learning progress across devices.

Send essential emails, such as sign-in codes. We do not send marketing emails.

Respond to your feedback and fix bugs.

Understand how the app is used so we can improve it (see Section 5).

Keep the Service secure, including preventing abuse and automated copying of our content.

Comply with the law.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

5. Analytics and Service Providers

Analytics. We use PostHog, a product analytics service, to collect usage data such as pages viewed, features used, clicks, session length, and how often people come back. For signed-in players 13 and older, this data is linked to your account ID and email address so we can understand how learning journeys develop over time. For Guest Mode players and players under 13, it is not linked to an account, a name, or an email address. We also use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page visits without using cookies. For more information, see https://posthog.com/privacy.

Service providers. We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, who may use it only to provide their services to us:

Supabase — database and account sign-in.

Vercel — hosting the website and page-visit statistics.

Cloudflare — content delivery (including word images and audio) and security.

Google — "Continue with Google" sign-in, if you choose to use it.

PostHog — usage analytics, as described above.

Resend — email delivery.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety and rights of our users or others, or as part of a transfer of the Service to a new operator, who would remain bound by this Privacy Policy.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage and a small number of cookies to run the app: to save Guest Mode progress, keep you signed in (including when you add the app to your home screen), and remember your language and learning settings. Before anyone signs in, we also remember on the device whether the player said they are under 13 (never their age), so the right sign-in options are shown. PostHog stores a random identifier in your browser for the analytics described in Section 5. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing meowmeowstar19@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We will delete your account, learning data, and feedback within 30 days, except where we must keep certain information to comply with the law. Backup copies are overwritten on a rolling basis and are fully removed within 90 days.

Analytics data (see Section 5) is kept for up to 24 months from the date it was collected, after which it is deleted or aggregated so that it can no longer be linked to an individual.

Accounts of players under 13 have no email address; a parent or guardian can ask us to delete them as described in Section 8.

Guest Mode data stays on your device until you clear it.

8. Children's Privacy (Players Under 13)

PlushieWord is for learners of all ages, including children. This section explains how we handle information from children under 13, as required by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We never ask a child for more information than is needed to learn.

How we know a player is under 13. Before anyone signs in, we ask how old they are. We use the answer only to decide which sign-in options to show and to apply the protections in this section. We keep only whether the player is under 13, on their own device, not on our servers, and we do not store the age itself.

Guest Mode. Children can learn in Guest Mode, which keeps progress only on their device and sends no personal information to us.

Username accounts. Instead of Google or email sign-in, players under 13 can create a username account with just a username and a password. We do not collect an email address, real name, phone number, photo, or location. We ask children to make up a nickname and not to use their real name, email address, or phone number as a username, and our sign-up blocks usernames that look like an email address or a phone number. Passwords are stored only in hashed form. A username account saves the same learning data described in Section 3 so the child's progress is kept, including any words the child adds themselves; please help your child not to type personal information there. These accounts are marked as belonging to a player under 13, and in-app feedback is turned off for them.

Nothing is made public. PlushieWord has no chat, messaging, public profiles, or sharing features. A child's username and learning data are never shown to other users.

Analytics. For players under 13 (username accounts, and devices where the player said they are under 13), the usage statistics described in Section 5 are not linked to an account, a name, or an email address, we do not build a profile of the player, and we do not record their sessions. We use these statistics only to understand how the app is working and to improve it.

Persistent identifiers. We and the service providers listed in Section 5 use a random account ID, sign-in tokens stored in cookies and local storage, a random analytics identifier, and IP addresses. For children, we use these only to support the internal operations of the app: keeping the child signed in, saving and syncing their progress, keeping the Service secure and preventing abuse (including the hashed IP records described in Section 3), and keeping the Service working well. We never use them for advertising, to build a profile of a child, or to contact a child, and we do not allow anyone else to do so.

"Ask a parent for help." If a child forgets their password, the sign-in page offers an "Ask a parent for help" form, where the child enters their username, a parent's or guardian's email address, and an optional message. We use that email address only to reply to that one request (for example, to help reset the password), and we delete it from the request once we have finished handling it. Please do not include other personal information in the message.

Sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share children's information. We disclose it only to the service providers listed in Section 5, who may use it only to run the Service for us, or when required by law.

Data retention. We keep a child's username account and learning data only so the child does not lose their progress. We delete the account and all of its learning data when a parent or guardian asks, or automatically if the account has not been used (no sign-in and no learning) for 36 months. Hashed IP records are deleted within 24 hours. A parent's email address from a help request is deleted once the request is handled, and the request itself is deleted after 36 months. Analytics data is kept for up to 24 months. Backup copies are fully removed within 90 days.

Parents' rights. A parent or guardian can at any time review the information we hold about their child's account, ask us to delete the account and its data, and refuse any further collection or use of the child's information (which we do by deleting the account). To do this, use "Ask a parent for help" on the sign-in page, or email us at meowmeowstar19@gmail.com with the child's username. Because these accounts have no email address, before acting we will ask you to tell us something only the account's owner would know (such as roughly when the account was created, or some words the child added or studied recently) to make sure we are helping the right family.

Other accounts. Google and email sign-in are for players 13 and older. If we learn that one of those accounts belongs to a child under 13, we will promptly delete it and its personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe this has happened, please contact us at meowmeowstar19@gmail.com.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

Access — ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate information.

Deletion — ask us to delete your information.

Portability — ask for your information in a machine-readable format.

Objection or restriction — object to or limit certain processing.

To use any of these rights, email us at meowmeowstar19@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days and will not treat you differently for using them. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

EEA / UK users. We process your information to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract), to understand and improve the app, keep the Service secure, and fix problems (legitimate interests, which never override your rights), and to comply with legal obligations.

California users. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we have not sold or shared the personal information of any consumer (including consumers under 16) in the past 12 months. We do not use sensitive personal information.

10. International Data Transfers

PlushieWord is available worldwide, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. For transfers of personal information from the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland to countries without an adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, where applicable) offered by our providers.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls that let each user read only their own data. No system is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.
